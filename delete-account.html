Account & User Data Deletion

Application: NMMS & CET Exam Portal (Android App: in.ponsrischool.nmms)
Developer / School: PM SHRI PRATHMIK VIDHYAMANDIR PONSRI

Users of the NMMS & CET Exam Portal app (including students, parents, and teachers) have the full right to request the complete deletion of their account profile and any associated cloud data stored in our database at any time.

1 Steps to Request Account & Data Deletion

To request permanent deletion of your account and related records, follow these simple steps:

Option 1: Send an Email Request (Recommended)

Send an email from your registered Google Account to our administration team:

  • To: patelsandip288@gmail.com or ponsri.big.gan.nav@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - NMMS Exam App
  • Content: Please include your registered email address and student name.

Option 2: In-App Logout & Local Reset

Within the mobile app: Go to Profile (પ્રોફાઇલ) → Click Sign Out (લૉગઆઉટ). To clear local attempt data, you can also clear app storage from Android Settings → Apps → NMMS Exam → Clear Storage.

2 What Data is Deleted and What is Retained

Permanently Deleted:

  • User's Google Account ID and email address
  • Student Display Name & Roll Number
  • All exam attempts, submission timestamps, and answers
  • Scores and leaderboard records associated with the user

Retained / Anonymized:

  • Only fully anonymized, aggregate question statistics (e.g. overall percentage of students who answered Question 5 correctly), with no personal identifiers whatsoever.

3 Processing Timeline & Retention Period

Once your deletion request email is received, our technical administrator will delete your user profile and Firestore database records within 24 to 48 hours. A confirmation email will be sent back to you once deletion is complete.
